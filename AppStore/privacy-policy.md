# Senso: Privacy Policy and Support

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Senso collects no personal data. Nothing you do in Senso is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

## Microphone

Senso listens only while you are pouring with it: after you tap Fill up, start it with Siri, the Action
Button or Control Center, or turn on "Start listening when Senso opens". It analyses the sound on your iPhone,
in real time, to tell how full your cup is. The audio is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

## Camera (optional, off by default)

If you turn on "Camera help to point at the cup", Senso analyses camera images on your iPhone before you pour
to tell you where the cup is. While you pour, it also takes the average colours of the picture to tint the
screen's background. The images are never saved or sent anywhere.

## Apple Watch

If you use the Senso Watch app, your iPhone and Apple Watch exchange the pour's progress, your stop level and
wrist movement using Apple's Watch Connectivity. This information goes only between your own devices.

## Your settings

Your cups, their calibration and your settings are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting Senso deletes them.

## No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking

Senso has no account and no advertising. It contains no analytics or third-party code and makes no network
requests. The only exception is a web link you tap yourself in Settings › Help › Acknowledgements, which opens in Safari.

## Children

Senso collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
