**Description** (4000 max)

Senso helps blind and low-vision people pour drinks without overflowing.

Put your iPhone next to the cup, tap Fill Up, or say "Start pouring with Senso", and pour. Senso listens to the sound of the liquid. As a cup fills, the pitch of the air above the liquid rises; Senso follows it, tells you how full the cup is, and says "Stop! Glass is full" early enough for you to stop.

• Spoken progress at 25, 50, 75 and 85%, through VoiceOver or Senso's own voice
• A clear stop sound, timed to give you time to react
• Apple Watch: taps on your wrist that speed up as the cup fills. Set your stop level with the Digital Crown.
• Calibrate your favourite cups once for a more accurate level
• Start hands-free with Siri, the Action Button or Control Center
• Optional camera to find your cup and help you point your iPhone at it
• Designed for VoiceOver and large text

Private by design: sound and camera images are analysed on your iPhone and never leave it. Senso has no account, no ads and no tracking, and collects no data.

Senso is an aid, not a guarantee. The level can be wrong, especially with cups you haven't calibrated, in noisy rooms or with thick liquids. Take extra care with hot drinks.
