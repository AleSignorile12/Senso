# Sensow: Privacy Policy and Support

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Sensow collects no personal data. Nothing you do in Sensow is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

## Microphone

Sensow listens only while you are pouring with it: after you tap Fill up, start it with Siri, the Action
Button or Control Center, or turn on "Start listening when Sensow opens". It analyses the sound on your iPhone,
in real time, to tell how full your cup is. The audio is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

## Camera (optional, off by default)

If you turn on "Use the camera to find the cup", Sensow analyses camera images on your iPhone to find your cup,
so it can recognise the pour a little sooner and tell you how to point your iPhone at it. While you pour, it
also takes the average colours of the picture to tint the screen's background. The images are never saved or
sent anywhere.

## Apple Watch

If you use the Sensow Watch app, your iPhone and Apple Watch exchange the pour's progress, your stop level and
wrist movement using Apple's Watch Connectivity. This information goes only between your own devices.

## Your settings

Your cups, their calibration and your settings are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting Sensow deletes them.

## No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking

Sensow has no account and no advertising. It contains no analytics or third-party code and makes no network
requests. The only exception is a web link you tap yourself in Settings › Help › Acknowledgements, which opens in Safari.

## Children

Sensow collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
